CNC Machine

Designed and built a custom CNC machine, including frame, axes, and spindle, for precise fabrication of metal components. Selected and integrated NEMA 23 stepper motors, drivers, microcontroller-based control system (Arduino) and switching power circuits. Implemented safety features including limit switches and an emergency stop functionality.

Power tools such as electric drills, saws, and grinders were used, along with measuring instruments to ensure correct construction and alignment.

The assembly process required hands-on work, alignment, and precise fitting.

Tinkering, problem solving and adjustments were made to ensure accurate assembly.
