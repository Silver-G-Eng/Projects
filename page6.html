Corner Camera Mount

The Corner Camera Mount solves a couple of issues; consumers have when installing outdoor monitoring/security systems. The product extends camera viewing angles. Decreases maintenance and allows upgrades to the system. Its design simplifies wiring installation by minimizing the need for drilling corner walls, ceilings, or tight structural areas.

The structure is manufactured from heat-resistant, low-deflection plastic.

Low cost with a modern and clean appearance for users.

Recyclable and non-toxic
