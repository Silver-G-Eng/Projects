Senior Design: Range Reminder System

Team of three designed a low power wireless device that tracks distance between devices and alerts once they exceed certain range.

1

The team managed to efficiently shrink the device’s size by 50% (70x40mm to 35x40mm).

2

Designed a switching power system and charging system that powers the device.

3

Implemented dynamic power management techniques that optimized power consumption by 96% (0.5W to 0.02W).
