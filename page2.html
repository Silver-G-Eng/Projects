I planned, managed and led a 22kW ASIC and GPU facility project. Calculated equipment power, current, continuous load requirements. Budgeted and sourced equipment and materials from different vendors. Sized PSUs, PDUs, breakers and conductors. Designed 3D physical layouts and power distribution layouts using CAD software. I also evaluated the facility power requirements to ensure a safe and efficient operation. Some other tasks performed were equipment maintenance, including cleaning, thermal paste replacement, and heatsink reattachment. Once in operation I troubleshooted issues, and optimized settings to balance stability of the equipment and its energy consumption. I coordinated with an electrical contractor to install the necessary electrical infrastructure needed to complete the project on schedule.
